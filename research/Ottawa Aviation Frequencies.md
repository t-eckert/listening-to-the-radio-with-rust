# Ottawa Aviation Frequencies

VHF airband frequencies for Ottawa Macdonald-Cartier International Airport (CYOW). All use AM modulation in the 118-137 MHz range.

Note: Ottawa is bilingual — pilots and controllers may communicate in English or French.

## CYOW Frequencies

| Frequency (MHz) | Service | Notes |
|-----------------|---------|-------|
| 118.8 | Tower | Active when planes are landing/departing |
| 121.9 | Ground | Taxiing aircraft |
| 121.3 | Clearance Delivery | Pre-departure routing |
| 119.2 | Terminal / Approach | Arriving aircraft |
| 124.1 | Terminal / Approach | Alternate sector |
| 127.7 | Terminal / Departure | Departing aircraft |
| 127.6 | ATIS (Arrival) | Continuous automated weather broadcast |
| 124.0 | ATIS (Departure) | Continuous automated weather broadcast |
| 121.5 | Emergency (Guard) | International emergency frequency, always monitored |

## Best for demos

- **118.8 (Tower)** — most likely to have voice traffic during operating hours
- **127.6 or 124.0 (ATIS)** — continuous automated broadcast, always on, good for testing
- **119.2 (Approach)** — commercial traffic arriving, tends toward English

## Antenna setup

- Quarter wavelength at 120 MHz is about 62 cm
- Extend the dipole to roughly 60 cm per side
- Aviation AM is vertically polarized — orient the dipole vertically if possible

## From Bayview Yards

The talk venue (7 Bayview Station Rd, 2nd floor) is approximately 11 km from YOW with a relatively clear path. VHF signals at these frequencies should propagate well at that distance.
