## Good choices for demos

- **106.1 (CHEZ)** — Classic rock, almost always playing music
- **100.3 (Majic)** — Pop, reliable audio content
- **91.5 (CBC)** — Talk radio, good for showing voice clarity
- **88.5 (Live)** — Alternative, strong signal at the low end of the band
